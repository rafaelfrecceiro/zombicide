At south exit five survivors in formation, active sixth survivor at south central junction outlined cyan; next central street zone has one walker, north junction contains a bulky fatty and lean runner; second walker is at active south junction. Matte black table with subtle printed comic framing. HUD has bold cream information cards and black text, angled red action tabs limited to headers, no noisy texture under small text; cyan outline selection and amber target area still consistent. Equipment art and survivor portraits are original expressive modern comics. Deliberately more graphic and stylized than a realistic tabletop. Premium PC game UI, not children's cartoon, no chibi, no pixel art, no giant comic slogans.
